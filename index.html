The filter burns off soot every few hundred miles ("regen", ~10–20 min of driving). Switching off mid-regen leaves it part-done; repeated interruptions are how DPFs block on short-trip diesels.

Fuel left & range

Reads the tank sensor once connected.

Litres left = fuel level (PID 2F) × tank size, smoothed over ~90 s so it doesn't jump with slosh. Range uses your last ~50 miles of trips (then this trip, then 45 mpg). Amber under 80 miles, red under 40. When the level jumps 10%+ while you're stationary the app logs a fill-up and asks for the receipt litres, which feeds the pump calibration.
